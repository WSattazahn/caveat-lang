## Ownership and required information

`apply_classified` clones a prospective session while retaining the old one for rollback ([reactive.rs:3493](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive.rs:3493)). The session shares many maps/vectors through Arc ([reactive.rs:1267](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive.rs:1267)); a first shared `make_mut` clones the **whole pointed-to container and its owned contents**, not only the changed entry. A later mutation may already be unique. State cells have finer sharing: Arc<Vec<Arc<StateCell>>> ([reactive.rs:759](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive.rs:759)), with individual replacements at line798. Successful assignment destroys old state; rejection destroys staged state in their measured cleanup phases. Thus allocations created during one phase may survive until another phase or beyond the event.

Exact still-required lineage, own grounds, withdrawal reasons, identities and undrained archive payloads are contract information. Their continued existence cannot be removed merely to meet a memory goal. Full-map copies, duplicate temporary names, container capacity, temporary serialization buffers and repeated representation of the same identities are distinct implementation costs. Atomic rollback requires preserving the old observable result, but does not by itself require every current coarse copy. No broader representation redesign is proposed here.

## Collection

Dirty owners rebuild reference sets, potentially detaching incoming/owner maps; unchanged sets already have a borrowed early comparison ([reactive_collector.rs:194](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive_collector.rs:194), [reactive_collector.rs:442](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive_collector.rs:442), [reactive_collector.rs:599](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive_collector.rs:599)). Candidate traversal creates owned-string BTreeSets for queued/considered/additional and per-region pending/region/roots/reachable state, cloning identities between them. Root and dependency traversal is required to establish conservative eligibility; these particular copies are temporary representations. Removing authoritative withdrawal reasons can detach that map and the derived multiplicity map at the end of collection.

One narrow candidate: avoid storing unreachable-region identities twice in `additional` and `considered`. Current code clones `region - reachable` into additional, then moves all region entries into considered ([reactive_collector.rs:698](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive_collector.rs:698)). A consuming partition into disjoint additional/considered sets could move those Strings, while the seed skip at line624 checks membership in their union. This preserves the graph walks and final sorted additional set in principle, but requires equivalence tests for revisited seeds, connected roots and cycles; actual work/peak counters must describe the changed temporary representation. The extra set lookup and effect on elapsed time remain unmeasured.

## ArchiveConstructionAndRemoval

The phase creates a complete node-id/name HashMap with cloned symbol Strings, a departing-id map with more identity clones, and a relations BTreeMap/Vec containing owned endpoint names ([reactive_departure.rs:823](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive_departure.rs:823)). It can detach the graph/symbol/observation/history/qualification maps; moves standing withdrawals into an extraction buffer; builds qualification maps and inline archive records in an entries BTreeMap; and materializes an inline-entry Vec for numeric history ordering before boxing records at publication ([reactive_departure.rs:866](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive_departure.rs:866), [reactive_departure.rs:995](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive_departure.rs:995)). Reading payloads and withdrawal records move into archive entries; renewal identity removals drop Strings. Collector index forgetting and scope-end buffer destruction also belong to this phase.

One narrow candidate: make the temporary id-to-name lookup borrow symbol names until all relation payloads are copied, then end that borrow before mutating symbols. Owned archive endpoint Strings must remain. This targets the full temporary symbol-name duplication rather than required archive information; Rust borrowing and the graph/symbol mutation boundary must be checked. It does not remove the HashMap allocation or guarantee a lower global peak.

## FinalSettlement

Changed Arc maps are selected by pointer comparison; their markers and binding/cue/archive provenance are traversed ([reactive_departure.rs:1047](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive_departure.rs:1047)). Settlement owns a nodes Vec and seen-digest BTreeSet. Pending DAG traversal creates a work Vec, clones seen IDs and published node payloads ([reactive_archive.rs:126](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive_archive.rs:126)). Content hashes currently serialize tuples into a temporary byte Vec ([reactive_archive.rs:72](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive_archive.rs:72)); record/union construction owns digest/history/parent Strings and pending Arc nodes. Every archived record also creates a record-anchor ArchiveTrace and immediately settles it ([reactive_departure.rs:1153](C:/Dev/caveat-lang/runtime/src/reactive_departure.rs:1153)). Published provenance is moved into the archive and remains until host drain. This phase includes final run_event-local cleanup, not solely DAG traversal.

One narrow candidate: directly publish/deduplicate that single-node record anchor through a shared canonical entry/identity builder, avoiding its temporary Pending Arc and one-element traversal representation. Preserve the exact existing digest bytes, entry fields, seen-ID behavior and node order; do not replace or omit DAG/record output. General DAG traversal and its iterative destruction should remain untouched. The impact is unmeasured and may be small compared with other settlement families.

## Interpretation limits

Wait for paired phase timing, requested-byte flows, simultaneous live-origin peak composition and correctness/equivalence results before choosing any task. High phase allocation does not identify a specific family, and high free traffic may dispose data allocated in a different phase or prior event. Distinguish required retained chain information, temporary/COW overhead, post-drain capacity and archive growth. None of these source observations establishes a general workload budget, bounded memory, or a predicted speed/heap reduction.
